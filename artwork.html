From Sketch to Finished Painting

I began the piece in Procreate, using its perspective tools to establish the architecture and composition before moving to Art Studio Pro for rendering.

I've been especially enjoying experimenting with strong beams of light in my Middle-earth paintings. They add dimensionality to the environment, and I'm continuing to explore how light can make a scene feel more three-dimensional.
